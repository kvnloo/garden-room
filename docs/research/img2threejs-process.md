## Five process insights

1. **Reference quality and specimen identity dominate.** Fragmented cutouts had encouraged generic plants and invented fullness. Native contextual crops show that the “aloe” is actually a tiny pruned strap-leaf specimen, and the broadleaf shrub is wilted with hanging folded leaves. Copy those visible states, not a healthy archetype. Keep background/neighbor exclusions explicit. Native crop boxes/source hashes are now locked in every `work/models/FAMILY/v2/target-lock.json`.
2. **A version label or “one prompt” is not a process receipt.** Only four demo-local ObjectSculptSpecs, plus the boxing-man pipeline spec, were found by the audit; the inventory records embedded assessments, detail inventories and review history separately. `git log` messages for Glock/Talon say “1 prompt/oneprompt” but do not recover the original dialogue. Twelve registry prompt fields are recoverable. Demand actual geometry-linked botanical detail inventory plus rendered evidence, not token-rich prose or retrospective claims.
3. **“Code-only” does not necessarily mean hand-authored procedural geometry.** Several newer organic demos use measured GLB surfaces, Surface Nets, embedded source buffers, textures or rig exports. The impressive detail is not proof that a cheap code model invented anatomy. `monster-tree/README.md` reports a historical 115,350-triangle surface and 41-bone export, far outside a 6,000-triangle mobile plant budget. Transfer measurement discipline and connected form, not asset scale or unexplained copied buffers.
4. **Architecture and leaf coupons must precede density/material.** The strongest transferable botanical source is `tropical-island/shrubs.ts`: a measured-shape tapered 3-column leaf ribbon, midrib crease, arch and droop, connected tapered petiole, coherent leaf orientation, subdued palette, and normals/shadows following wind. Its source is a useful example, not a license grant to copy. Crucially, current `coffee-house/foliage.ts` is **PixiJS**, deforming contextual image crops with a 12×14 grid; it is NOT an independent Three.js leaf generator. The old builder brief's coffee-house exemplar was therefore misleading for this contract.
5. **Retention needs controls, opposing judges and growth-specific evidence.** The old preview auto-fits each model and reports pot+plant triangles, hiding proportion changes and confusing budget checks. The new fixture locks camera/lighting from requested parameters, counts plant-only geometry, captures front/three/side/top plus 15% sapling and repeat control, and fingerprints deterministic geometry. A candidate must strictly improve the fixed multi-view judge score, win two blind swapped dream-loop A/B sessions, survive technical/sapling checks, and pass direct sheet inspection. More triangles or a flattering hero view cannot win alone.

## What the organic demos actually do

- **Tropical Island** — `src/demos/tropical-island/shrubs.ts` builds 9 lengthwise leaf segments with three vertices per row: sinusoidal width/arch/rib and trailing droop. Stems use six tapered rings/five sides; clusters merge leaf/petiole geometry rather than requiring one mesh per leaf. `SHRUB_WIND_GLSL` applies anchored slow gust + tip ripple; bent normals come from finite differences; depth/distance materials apply the same displacement; bounds are inflated. Leaf colour is muted HSL, central-vein modulation uses UV, and backlighting reads actual directional lights. `palmWind.ts` applies frond wind separately. This is a good craft reference because anatomical shape, motion, normals and shadows agree—not because “wind” makes any plant better. [Source](https://github.com/img2threejs/img2threejs-showcase/tree/main/src/demos/tropical-island).
- **Coffee House / Moonlight Cafe** — `foliage.ts` imports PixiJS Container/Mesh/MeshGeometry/Texture. Two rectangular context crops are deformed by weighted grid vertices; comments explicitly deny recovered individual leaf mattes. The showcase top-level README documents its dedicated PixiJS hash-route integration. This route is appropriate for 2D image-preserving atmosphere, not orbitable garden-room assets. [Source](https://github.com/img2threejs/img2threejs-showcase/blob/main/src/demos/coffee-house/foliage.ts).
- **Monster Tree** — `README.md`, `GROOT_STREAMING.md`, `object-sculpt-spec.json`, source provenance, rig and pooled effects distinguish geometry source, present animation and historical experiments. The README explains measured bind/inverse-bind, elapsed/delta and rigid-accessory failures; it explicitly says export geometry was already measured, not re-sculpted. This documentation quality is worth copying. Historical triangle counts are not the current full scene budget. [README](https://github.com/img2threejs/img2threejs-showcase/blob/main/src/demos/monster-tree/README.md).
- **Snowy Village** — registry claims Hyper3D, native Three.js role modules and original PBR image bytes; `native/manifest.json` and native source modules preserve provenance/material data. That is not a no-assets leaf recipe. Any source triangle budgets are listed in the audit, otherwise unknown. [Source](https://github.com/img2threejs/img2threejs-showcase/tree/main/src/demos/snowy-village).
- **Other organic/character forms** — girl-character, mars-cat, warrior, leesin and related registry entries cite Surface Nets, measured reference meshes or retargeted rigs; man-robot-studio uses serialized ObjectLoader source. They demonstrate reconstruct→measure→render→correct, not a general small procedural plant generator. Per-demo geometry/material/instancing/projection evidence is in the 32-row appendix.

## Canonical standard prompts (read, extracted verbatim)

`docs/standard-prompts/{README,build,glb-force-measured,polish,vfx}.md` exists in the **skill** checkout, not in the showcase. **Reuse chosen:** vendored EZ-Tree source pinned to `dcf309bd86bd521083d9c70f01f2de45fdc7c457` under `public/models/plants/_shared/ez-tree/`, preserving MIT LICENSE and `PROVENANCE.json`. Banana, pruned strap succulent, seedlings and lax trailing vines are anatomical exceptions, not excuses to hand-roll another generic branching tree.

**Additional image/procedural approaches:** [microsoft/TRELLIS](https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS) has 13,999 stars and MIT source (fetched API snapshot); its generated GLB can be a build-time measurement instrument but is not directly admissible here. [simondevyoutube/LSystems_JavaScript](https://github.com/simondevyoutube/LSystems_JavaScript) has 52 stars/MIT: useful educational turtle grammar, less production-ready than EZ-Tree's reusable skeleton. [msiric/procedural-plants](https://github.com/msiric/procedural-plants) has 9 stars, API licence null although README claims MIT; [sneha-belkhale/procedural-plants-threejs](https://github.com/sneha-belkhale/procedural-plants-threejs) has 6 stars, API licence null, older shader-art examples; neither is chosen. Metadata is saved in `work/research/oss-extra.json`. [Learning to Infer Parameterized Representations of Plants from 3D Scans](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.22337v3) describes a learned recursive/L-system parameterization and discusses multi-image CropCraft; its scan/training requirements do not solve these single cluttered photos cheaply. Parameterized anatomy plus target-derived branch occupancy is the practical image-to-procedural route for this batch.

**Leaf cards:** alpha-tested cards are a standard way to avoid sorted-transparent overlap problems ([Three.js transparency manual](https://threejs.org/manual/en/transparency.html)). Crossed cards are efficient distant canopy impostors but collapse or reveal quads at top/side views. Under this no-external-assets contract, actual silhouette ribbons with shallow 3D folds are safer. Generated alpha masks could be legal but cannot fix wrong shape/architecture. Budget counts multiplicity, not just base card geometry.

**Space colonization:** Runions, Lane & Prusinkiewicz, [Modeling Trees with a Space Colonization Algorithm (2007)](https://algorithmicbotany.org/papers/colonization.egwnp2007.pdf), uses crown-envelope attraction points, nearest branches, influence/kill distances and tropism to explain branching. Good future route for target-derived canopy masks. It does not recover leaves/species automatically; naive spherical attractors recreate generic bushes. EZ-Tree's existing parameterized skeleton is lower-risk for this batch.

**Botany / phyllotaxis:** [University of Tokyo, Mathematics of leaves](https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/focus/en/press/z0508_00047.html) distinguishes alternate vs whorled patterns, distichous 180°, decussate 90° pairs and Fibonacci 137.5° spirals. Do not apply the golden angle to every species. Basil/plectranthus need opposite/decussate nodes; curry leaf needs a compound rachis with leaflet pairs; tomato's compound leaf is not a whorl of unrelated simple leaves. Species identification remains uncertain from some photos; copy visible morphology and label inference.

## Concrete improved process / builder prompt

Executable staged contract and full copyable prompt: **`work/builders/BRIEF_v2.md`**. Canonical scoped preferences: `work/builders/OWNER-PREFERENCES_v2.md`.

1. Native crop + exclusion/identity reading → immutable target manifest/hashes.
2. Reproduce no-change adult 4-view + sapling baseline and save actual critic JSON.
3. Structured intake/detail inventory → two form hypotheses, negative example, one declared physical hypothesis.
4. Connected architecture using MIT generator where appropriate → leaf coupon → instance with species attachment rules → age-dependent topology.
5. Syntax/runtime/plant-only budget/finite/deterministic checks before vision.
6. Fixed multi-view absolute judge + native visual comparison + two fresh swapped dream-loop A/B trials. The identical-image calibration must tie; it did in both native sessions (`work/models/v2-calibration`). This is a limited same-image/side-bias sanity check, not full owner-labelled ranker calibration.
7. Parent retained on tie, disagreement, missing evidence or technical/growth regression. Strict wins only; copy exact judged source and preserve approved_prev. At most six candidate judge rounds/family; same blocker after two focused trials stops for a changed mechanism.

## Batch result (verified, not a promise)

See [`plant-quality-v2-results.md`](plant-quality-v2-results.md) and `work/models/v2-summary.json`. The generic-generator rewrite mostly failed: one family met promotion, others remain their original parent. EZ-Tree is the chosen reusable skeleton, **not a demonstrated universal botanical quality win**. Actual leaf-frame coupons and measured target crown occupancy are required before repeating bulk generation. The first tomato builder note wrongly asserted fruit absence despite green fruit in the crop; this error is explicitly retained in results. BRIEF_v2 now requires a real coordinator-approved coupon checkpoint rather than accepting a text assertion.

### Known evidence boundaries
- Adult contextual reference ≠ juvenile reference. Sapling judges assess biological plausibility, not claimed ground-truth growth reconstruction.
- A/B front views do not prove alternate-view fidelity. Multi-view critic and runtime capture supply separate evidence.
- No global image warp/SSIM hard gate is calibrated on these heterogeneous photos; backgrounds, pots and viewpoints differ. Structural inspection remains explicit; do not manufacture a photo-pixel PASS.
- Self-consistency and human/vision critic wins select an improvement, not photorealism. No demo-source copying is authorized by a missing licence.
